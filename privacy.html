Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

Too Far Stories Uploader is a private production tool used only by the owner of the Too Far? YouTube channel. It is not offered as a service to the public.

Data accessed

With the owner's explicit Google authorization, the tool may read the identity and basic metadata of the authorized YouTube channel and upload videos to that channel. It requests only the YouTube read-only and upload permissions needed for those functions.

Data storage

OAuth credentials are stored in restricted local or server configuration. They are not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for profiling. Operational records may store video IDs, upload status, titles, and technical logs; passwords and access tokens are excluded from logs.

Data sharing

Data is sent only to Google/YouTube as necessary to authorize the owner, read the authorized channel, and perform owner-requested uploads. The tool does not transfer Google user data to unrelated third parties.

Retention and deletion

Credentials are retained while the private automation remains active. The owner can revoke access at any time through Google Account permissions. Stored credentials and operational records can then be removed from the controlled systems.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to the developer contact address displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.

This application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
